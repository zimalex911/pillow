How to drop your 20 poses

  1. Use the Load pillow image button above to pick the exact pillow photo you provided. It swaps instantly into the center.
  2. Click Load 20 poses, select your twenty pose files at once (JPG/PNG/SVG). They load in filename order into the clock, glide to center for ~1s, hold for ~1s, then return — all within a 30s full loop.
  3. If you prefer pre-wiring assets, drop them into assets/poses/pose-01.ext … pose-20.ext and your pillow into assets/pillow-center.ext; reload the page.
  4. Toggle auto-play with the checkbox to pause/resume the sequence; Reset clears focus and stops the loop.
