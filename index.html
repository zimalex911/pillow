How to swap in your assets

  1. Replace assets/pillow.svg with {{путь_к_изображению_подушки}} (keep it roughly 4:3 or 3:2 so it sits comfortably in the center).
  2. In script.js update the characters array with {{пути_к_изображениям_персонажей}}. Ideal size: square-ish PNG/SVG, 200–320 px, consistent stroke/flat style.
  3. For photo sources, run a quick vectorize/filter pass (e.g., "posterize" or "cartoon" filter) to keep everything in a soft, friendly style.
